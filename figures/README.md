## Key Visualizations

### Policy Stance Across Governance Levels

![Mean GenAI policy stance across governance tiers](figures/mean-stance-by-governance-tier.png)

GenAI policies generally became more restrictive as governance moved closer to the classroom. The pattern was especially pronounced in New York City.

### Policy Stance by Governance Tier and Region

![Policy stance distribution by governance tier and region](figures/stance-distribution-by-tier-region.png)

The largest differences between New York City and Hong Kong appeared at the city/territory and course levels.

### Institutional Differences in GenAI Policy

![Policy stance by institution](figures/policy-stance-by-institution.png)

Hong Kong institutions showed relatively similar policy-stance distributions, while New York City institutions exhibited greater variation.
